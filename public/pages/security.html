How We Protect
Your Data

We're a security company, so we hold ourselves to a high bar — and we believe in showing our work, not hiding behind badges. Here's exactly how your data is protected.

🔐 Mandatory 2FA on every account
🔒 AES-256 encryption
🧪 Tested against the OWASP Top 10
📢 Responsible disclosure program
🔒

Encryption

  • Files encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM — tamper-evident storage
  • All traffic encrypted in transit over HTTPS / TLS
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt — never stored in plain text
🔑

Account Security

  • Two-factor authentication is required on every account
  • Authenticator-app (TOTP) sign-in with one-time backup codes
  • New-location sign-ins require an emailed verification code
  • Passwords checked against known breach databases at sign-up
  • Automatic lockout after repeated failed login attempts
👁️

Access & Visibility

  • Role-based access — admins and standard users
  • Files belong to your account; only admins see everything
  • Every file action is logged with user, time, and IP
  • See and sign out active devices any time
💾

Backups & Recovery

  • Automatic backups protect against data loss
  • Deleted files sit in a recoverable Trash for 30 days
  • Stored on cloud infrastructure built for high durability
🛡️

Monitoring & Hardening

  • Email scanned for phishing and scams (every plan)
  • Rate limiting blocks brute-force and abuse
  • Uploads validated by content, and risky file types blocked
  • Security headers and a strict content policy
🔗

Safe Sharing

  • Share links expire automatically (you choose when)
  • Optional password protection on any shared link
  • Revoke a link instantly — access stops immediately

Being honest about where we are: We're a new company and are not yet certified against formal frameworks like SOC 2, ISO 27001, or HIPAA. The controls above are real and active, and they're the same building blocks those frameworks require — but we'd rather tell you plainly than imply a certification we don't hold. As we grow, pursuing formal certification is on our roadmap.

Our Commitments

What You Can Expect From Us

  • Your data is yours. Export or permanently delete your files at any time.
  • Least-privilege access. Internal access is limited to the minimum needed to run the service.
  • Prompt breach notification. If your data is ever affected, we'll tell you quickly and clearly.
  • Fast patching. We monitor our software dependencies and fix known vulnerabilities promptly.
  • Ongoing security testing. The app is tested against common web attacks (the OWASP Top 10) as we build.
  • Transparency over badges. We tell you plainly what we do and don't have — no security theater.
Responsible Disclosure

Found a Security Issue?

We take security reports seriously and appreciate the people who help keep our customers safe. If you've found a vulnerability, please tell us privately before disclosing it publicly, and we'll work with you to fix it quickly.
