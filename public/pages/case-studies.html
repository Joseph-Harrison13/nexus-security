Healthcare · Dental & Medical

Dental & Medical Offices

Patient records · scheduling · imaging
Common Risks

Many small practices run on aging computers with patient records, X-rays, and scheduling data stored on a single office machine — often with no offsite backup. That's exactly the setup ransomware preys on, and losing patient data is both a business and a privacy disaster.

How We Help

We set up automatic encrypted backups with 30 days of history so a ransomware hit or hardware failure doesn't mean lost records. We add two-factor authentication, scan incoming email for phishing, and give you clear access logs. Strong encryption and audit trails are useful building blocks if you're working toward HIPAA — though we don't certify compliance.

⚖️
Legal · Small Law Firms

Law Firms & Practices

Confidential case files · multi-user access
Common Risks

Attorneys often share confidential case files over personal email or consumer file-sharing tools — with no way to know who accessed what, and no audit trail if something goes missing. For sensitive legal matters, that's a real exposure.

How We Help

We give each person role-based access so they only see the files they need, log every file open and download with a timestamp and user, and require two-factor authentication on every account. Files are encrypted at rest, and you get a clear, exportable record of all activity.

🍕
Hospitality · Restaurants & Retail

Multi-Location Businesses

Payroll · vendor contracts · records
Common Risks

A small management team often juggles payroll, vendor contracts, and records across personal laptops and shared consumer drives. When a device is lost or stolen, there's usually no way to know what business files were on it — or to cut off access.

How We Help

We move shared files into encrypted cloud storage so data isn't trapped on personal devices. Access can be revoked for any user instantly, files are organized by location for easy access, and everything is backed up automatically every day.
