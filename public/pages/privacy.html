Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  Effective: June 1, 2026

We keep this simple: we collect only what we need to run your account, we never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it anytime.

1. Who We Are

Nexus Security, LLC ("Nexus Security", "we", "us", or "our") provides cybersecurity and cloud protection services to small businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly and information generated when you use our services:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored encrypted) when you create an account.
  • Contact form submissions — your name, email, business name, and message when you contact us.
  • Files you upload — documents and data you store through our cloud storage service.
  • Usage data — login times, IP addresses, and activity logs for security monitoring and fraud prevention.
  • Device information — browser type and operating system to optimize your experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain your account and cloud storage
  • Send you security alerts and account notifications
  • Respond to your support requests and messages
  • Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and security threats
  • Improve our services based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers we control and is protected by:

  • AES-256 encryption at rest
  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit
  • Multi-factor authentication requirements
  • Automatic encrypted backups retained for 7 days
  • Access controls limiting which staff can view your data

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Session cookies — to keep you logged in during your session (required for the service to work)
  • Preference cookies — to remember your consent choices

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this will prevent you from logging in.

6. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you close your account:

  • Your files are deleted within 30 days
  • Account records are retained for 90 days in case of disputes, then deleted
  • Security logs (IP addresses, login times) are retained for 12 months for fraud prevention

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate information
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and associated data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a notice on our website at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

Questions about this policy?

Contact us at our contact page or email us directly. We aim to respond to all privacy inquiries within 48 hours.
