  • Your files live in durable cloud storage, with automatic backups protecting your data against hardware failure

Around-the-Clock Protection

Automated monitoring keeps an eye on your accounts day and night, helping you spot suspicious sign-ins and scam emails before they become a problem.

  • If we spot a suspicious sign-in or account event, you get an email or in-app alert right away
  • We notice when someone signs in from an unfamiliar location and alert you immediately
  • Uploads are checked and risky file types are blocked automatically

Control Who Sees What

You decide exactly which employees can see which files — keeping sensitive information private and in the right hands.
